\chapter{Introduction}

With this app we aim to be able to bring a new insightful perspective to the Austrian science and technology funding landscape.
This analysis platform should connect companies, universities, and other research institutions through their common projects and fields of research.
By connecting the project funding we wanted to show industry trend, research gaps and funding concentrations with publicly available data.


This project was developed in collaboration with my colleague Bastian Kandlbauer, B.Sc., who took over
development of the front-facing dashboard and its visualization while I focused backend-facing data
gathering aspect. This division of labour was indispensable for the timely and successful completion of the project.

\section{Research Questions}

\todo{Expand}

Main: How can a configurable data pipeline combine heterogeneous research-funding data into a consistent, enriched
dataset that non-developers can maintain and adapt to change?

Data generation: What data quality problems arise when combining FFG and FWF data on projects, organizations and
funding, and how can they be overcome with publicly available data?

Architecture: Which architecture lets data pipelines be configured, scheduled, monitored and versioned through a web
interface?

Adaptability and usability: To what extent can domain experts run the pipeline, publish datasets and configure it to
changing source APIs without developer support?
